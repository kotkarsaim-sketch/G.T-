Noise Control By-law No. 2024-112

Being a by-law of The Corporation of the City of Rivermont to prohibit and regulate excessive noise within the municipality. Council authority: Municipal Government Act, section 7(2). Read a first time on 2024-05-13, second time on 2024-06-03, third time and finally passed on 2024-06-17. Effective 2024-07-01, replacing By-law 2016-009 as it related to noise.

1. Definitions
In this By-law: "dwelling unit" has the meaning given in the Animal Control By-law, being any building or part of a building used as a permanent residence; "quiet hours" means the period from 22:00 on one day to 07:00 the following day, local time; "residential zone" means land zoned R-1, R-2, R-3 or RM under the zoning map in force from time to time; "construction activity" means erection, demolition, alteration or repair of any structure, including loading and unloading of equipment and materials.

2. General prohibition
No person shall make, cause or permit any noise in the municipality that unreasonably disturbs the peace of other persons, having regard to the type, intensity, duration and time of the noise and the character of the surrounding area. For clarity, the following are presumed unreasonable when audible from another dwelling unit: amplified music between quiet hours; persistent animal noise lasting more than fifteen minutes; operation of power tools between quiet hours; and vehicle engine idling in excess of five minutes while stationary, except emergency vehicles.

3. Construction activity
Construction activity is permitted only between 07:00 and 20:00 on weekdays and between 09:00 and 18:00 on Saturdays, and is prohibited on Sundays and statutory holidays except in an emergency. A person may apply in writing to the Chief Administrative Officer for a permit to conduct construction activity outside these hours where delay would create a safety hazard; the fee is set out in the annual fees schedule.

4. Quiet hours exceptions
Section 2 does not apply to noise arising from an emergency, works carried out by or on behalf of the municipality, or an event licensed by the municipality where the licence authorizes extended hours.

5. Exemptions - agricultural and domestic animals
Nothing in this By-law prohibits the keeping of bees in accordance with the Animal Control By-law, provided that hives are located at least five metres from any property line and a water source is maintained on the property. Machinery used in normal farm practice is exempt.

6. Enforcement and offences
A by-law enforcement officer may issue a warning or a penalty notice. Every person who contravenes this By-law is guilty of an offence and liable on first conviction to a fine of not more than $2,000, and on subsequent convictions to a fine of not more than $5,000 or imprisonment for a term not exceeding thirty days, or both. Continued contravention constitutes a separate offence for each day it occurs.

7. Penalties schedule
The fixed penalty amounts set under section 6 are: amplified music during quiet hours, $250; power tools during quiet hours, $200; construction activity contrary to section 3, $500; vehicle idling contrary to section 2, $150. Fixed penalties double for a second offence within twelve months.

8. Severability
Should any provision of this By-law be found invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, that provision is severed and the remainder continues in force.

9. Repeal
By-law 2016-009 is repealed effective 2024-07-01 to the extent it regulates noise. Nothing in that repeal affects anything lawfully done under it before that date.

10. Effective date
This By-law comes into force on 2024-07-01.
