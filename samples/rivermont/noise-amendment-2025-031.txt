Noise Control Amendment By-law No. 2025-031

Being a by-law of The Corporation of the City of Rivermont to amend the Noise Control By-law No. 2024-112. Read three times and finally passed 2026-08-12. Awaiting publication by the Clerk.

1. Penalties amended
Section 7 of By-law 2024-112 is amended by replacing the fixed penalty for amplified music during quiet hours with $300, effective on the date this By-law comes into force. All other fixed penalties in section 7 remain unchanged.

2. Effective date
This By-law comes into force on the day it is published by the Clerk.
