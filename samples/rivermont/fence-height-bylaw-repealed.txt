Fence and Structure Height By-law No. 2016-009

(REPEALED in its entirety effective 2024-07-01 by By-law 2024-112, section 9. Retained in the corpus for historical questions only; this text was not the current law after that date.)

Being a former by-law of The Corporation of the City of Rivermont regulating the height and location of fences and accessory structures. Passed 2016-03-21, effective 2016-05-01, repealed 2024-07-01.

1. Definitions
In this By-law: "fence" means a barrier erected outdoors for enclosure, screening or decoration, excluding hedges and retaining walls under 0.6 metres; "corner lot" has the meaning in the zoning map then in force.

2. Maximum heights
Fences within a required front yard could not exceed 1.0 metre; fences within any other yard could not exceed 2.0 metres; fences on corner lots abutting a flanking street could not exceed 1.2 metres within 3 metres of the property line.

3. Materials and finish
Fences were required to be constructed of wood, metal, masonry or vinyl, with the finished side facing outward toward neighbouring properties or streets.

4. Accessory structures
Sheds and similar accessory structures could not exceed 3.5 metres in height nor cover more than 12 percent of the lot area, and required a permit above 10 square metres of floor area.

5. Variances
The Committee of Adjustment could grant a variance of up to 25 percent of a maximum height in section 2 where compliance would cause practical difficulty without adding neighbour benefit.
